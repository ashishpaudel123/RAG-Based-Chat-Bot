# Warranty and Technical Support

## Warranty Coverage

All products sold by Himal Electronics come with the official manufacturer warranty. Mobile phones carry a 1-year warranty, laptops carry a 1-year warranty (2 years for selected business models), televisions carry a 2-year warranty on the panel, and large home appliances such as refrigerators carry a 10-year warranty on the compressor and 1 year on other parts.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover

The warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, damage caused by voltage fluctuations without a stabiliser, unauthorised repairs, or normal wear such as battery capacity loss after 6 months. Accessories such as chargers and cables carry a 6-month warranty.

## Making a Warranty Claim

To make a warranty claim, bring the product, the invoice and the warranty card to any Himal Electronics service centre. Service centres are located in New Road (Kathmandu), Lakeside (Pokhara) and Traffic Chowk (Butwal). Service centres are open from 10 AM to 6 PM, Sunday to Friday. Repairs usually take 7 to 15 working days depending on part availability.

## Extended Warranty

Customers can buy the Himal Care extended warranty within 30 days of purchase. Himal Care adds one extra year of coverage and costs 8% of the product price. It also includes one free accidental screen repair for phones and laptops.

## Technical Support

Free technical support is available by phone at 01-5550123 and by email at support@himal-electronics.example. Support covers device setup, software updates and basic troubleshooting.
