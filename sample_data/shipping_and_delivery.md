# Shipping and Delivery Information

## Delivery Areas and Times

Himal Electronics delivers to all 77 districts of Nepal. Orders inside Kathmandu Valley (Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur) are delivered within 1 to 2 working days. Orders to Pokhara, Chitwan, Butwal, Biratnagar and other major cities are delivered within 3 to 5 working days. Remote areas may take 7 to 10 working days.

## Delivery Charges

Delivery is free for orders above Rs. 5,000 inside Kathmandu Valley. Orders below Rs. 5,000 inside the valley have a delivery charge of Rs. 100. Delivery outside the valley costs Rs. 250 for standard items and Rs. 500 for large appliances such as refrigerators, televisions above 43 inches and washing machines.

## Order Tracking

Once an order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email with a tracking number. You can track the order from "My Orders" in your account. Tracking information may take up to 12 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Cash on Delivery

Cash on delivery (COD) is available for orders up to Rs. 50,000. For orders above Rs. 50,000, full payment must be made online through card, eSewa, Khalti or bank transfer before dispatch.

## Failed Deliveries

Our delivery partner will attempt delivery twice. If both attempts fail because the customer is unreachable, the order is returned to the warehouse and cancelled. Prepaid orders cancelled this way are refunded minus the delivery charge.

## Changing the Delivery Address

The delivery address can be changed free of charge before the order is dispatched by contacting customer support. After dispatch, address changes are not possible.
