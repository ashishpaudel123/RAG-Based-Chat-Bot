# Account and Payments FAQ

## Creating an Account

You can create an account using your email address or mobile number. A verification code is sent by SMS or email and is valid for 10 minutes.

## Resetting Your Password

Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your registered email or mobile number. You will receive a reset link that expires after 30 minutes. For security, our staff will never ask for your password or verification code.

## Accepted Payment Methods

We accept Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards, eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS, bank transfer and cash on delivery for eligible orders.

## EMI (Instalment) Payments

EMI is available on purchases above Rs. 25,000 using credit cards from partner banks. EMI plans are available for 3, 6, 9 and 12 months. Zero-interest EMI is offered on selected products during promotional periods; otherwise the bank's interest rate applies.

## Loyalty Points

Customers earn 1 Himal Point for every Rs. 100 spent. Each Himal Point is worth Rs. 1 and can be used on future purchases. Points expire 12 months after they are earned and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Cancelling an Order

Orders can be cancelled free of charge before dispatch from "My Orders". Once an order is dispatched it cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse delivery or request a return according to the Returns and Refunds Policy.

## Invoices

A VAT invoice is emailed automatically after delivery. You can also download invoices from "My Orders". For a company (PAN) invoice, add your company PAN number at checkout; invoices cannot be changed after they are issued.
