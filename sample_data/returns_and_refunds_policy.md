# Returns and Refunds Policy

This policy applies to all purchases made from Himal Electronics (a fictional store used for this project) through our website, mobile app or physical showrooms in Nepal.

## Return Window

Most products can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Mobile phones, tablets and laptops can be returned within 7 days of delivery only if the device is unopened or has a manufacturing defect. Items bought during the Dashain and Tihar festival sale can be returned within 7 days.

## Conditions for a Return

To be eligible for a return, the item must be in its original packaging with all accessories, manuals and free gifts included. The original invoice or order number is required. Items that show physical damage, water damage or signs of misuse cannot be returned.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned: opened earphones and earbuds (for hygiene reasons), software licences and gift cards, printer ink and toner once opened, and products marked as "Final Sale".

## How to Start a Return

Log in to your account, open "My Orders", select the item and click "Request Return". You can also call our support line at 01-5550123 between 9 AM and 6 PM, Sunday to Friday. A pickup will be scheduled within 2 working days inside Kathmandu Valley; customers outside the valley must send the item through an approved courier and keep the receipt.

## Refunds

After the returned item passes inspection, the refund is processed within 5 working days. Refunds are made to the original payment method: card payments are refunded to the card, eSewa and Khalti payments are refunded to the same wallet, and cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer. Delivery charges are refunded only if the item was defective or we sent the wrong product.

## Exchanges

If you received a defective or wrong item, you may choose an exchange instead of a refund. Exchanges depend on stock availability. If the replacement is out of stock, a full refund will be issued.
